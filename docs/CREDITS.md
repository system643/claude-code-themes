## Original themes

Digital Rain, Phosphor, Arcade, Orbit, Abyss, Sakura, Lo-fi Café, Volcano, Christmas, Halloween, Neon 2087, Aurora and Grimoire are original creations of this repository: palettes, scenes, pixel art, sounds and texts.

## City themes

Paris, Tokyo, New York, London, Dubai, Rome, Seoul, Sydney, Rio de Janeiro and Singapore: the pixel art and palettes are original (`scripts/art/cities.py`); their wallpapers are photographs, credited below.

## Assets

The sprites, sounds and the remaining wallpapers (Digital Rain, Phosphor, Arcade, One Dark, Synthwave) are generated by the scripts in `scripts/art/` (Python, Pillow, NumPy). The other wallpapers are the photographs listed above; no other third-party images or sounds are included.
